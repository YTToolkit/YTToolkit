What is YTToolkit?

YTToolkit is a small collection of free tools built for YouTube creators who want faster workflows. If you’re writing titles, picking tags, or trying to package your video better, these tools help you generate ideas quickly.

Everything runs in the browser. No sign-up. No installs. Just open a tool, enter your topic, and copy what you need.

Tools included

  • YouTube Title Generator: generate multiple title ideas in different styles (how-to, curiosity, list, etc.).
  • YouTube Tags Generator: generate relevant tags based on a topic, with different tag styles and tag counts.

How to use

  1. Open any tool from the buttons above.
  2. Enter your video topic (be specific).
  3. Choose a style (optional), then generate results.
  4. Copy the best options and tweak them to match your video.

Tips for better results

  • Use clear topics like “how to edit reels in capcut” instead of “editing”.
  • Add context: platform, audience, and goal (views, tutorial, review).
  • Don’t copy blindly. Pick 1–2 good ideas and make them more specific.
