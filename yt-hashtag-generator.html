YouTube Hashtag Generator

Generate clean, relevant hashtags for YouTube Shorts, Reels, and videos. Click a hashtag to copy it.

How to use this tool

  • Type your video topic (be specific).
  • Choose a mode (Balanced, Niche, or Viral).
  • Click Generate, then copy the best hashtags.
  • Use 3–5 hashtags in your description (not 20).

Hashtag tips (avoid mistakes)

  • Use relevant hashtags only (random tags can hurt trust).
  • Mix broad + specific (example: #YouTubeTips + #YouTubeShortsStrategy).
  • Avoid banned/unsafe topics and excessive repetition.
