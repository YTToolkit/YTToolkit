How to use the YouTube Tags Generator

  • Enter your topic (example: “rubik’s cube”, “youtube shorts growth”).
  • Choose a mode: Balanced for normal use, SEO for long-tail keywords, Viral for trend-style tags.
  • Select how many tags you want and click Generate Tags.
  • Click any tag to copy it, or use Copy All Tags.

Tip: Keep your topic specific. “YouTube growth for gaming” gives better tags than just “YouTube”.

Where to use these tags

  • YouTube “Tags” field (if available in your upload flow).
  • In your description as natural keywords (don’t spam).
  • As ideas for your title, thumbnail text, and video chapters.

Tag tips (avoid mistakes)

  • Stay relevant: irrelevant tags can hurt performance.
  • Mix broad + specific: combine topic tags with long-tail tags.
  • Don’t overdo trends: only use viral tags if your video matches.
  • Use 15–30 max: quality beats quantity.

FAQ

Do tags still matter in 2026?

They matter less than title + thumbnail + retention, but tags still help with misspellings and context.

Why are some tags repeated or similar?

Because tags include close keyword variations. If you want more variety, change your topic wording.

Should I use commas or hashtags?

For tags, use comma-separated text in the tags field. Hashtags are better for descriptions.
