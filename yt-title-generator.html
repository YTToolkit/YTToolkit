How to use the YouTube Title Generator

  • Type your video topic in the box above.
  • Select a style (How-to, Authority, Curiosity, Fast/Quick, Mistakes).
  • Click Generate Titles and tap any title to copy it.

Tip: If your topic is too broad, add a niche or audience (example: “YouTube growth for gaming channels”).

Title writing tips (that actually help CTR)

  • Be specific: add numbers, time, or a clear result (example: “in 7 days”).
  • Use a simple promise: what will the viewer get after watching?
  • Match the thumbnail: your title and thumbnail must say the same story.
  • Avoid clickbait: make it curious, but still true.

Examples

  • Topic: “YouTube shorts” → “Grow on YouTube Shorts (Step-by-Step)”
  • Topic: “Rubik’s cube” → “Solve Rubik’s Cube the Right Way”
  • Topic: “video editing” → “Most People Learn Video Editing the Wrong Way”

FAQ

Does this tool guarantee viral results?

No. It generates title ideas. Your video quality, retention, thumbnail, and topic demand still matter.

How many titles should I test?

Pick 3–5 good ones, then choose the clearest title that matches your thumbnail and video.

Why do some titles look similar?

Because styles use patterns. Change your topic wording to get more variety.
